Process Pipeline

How BarangayDocs Works

From registration to claims, our streamlined government process guarantees efficiency at every turn.

01

Online Request

Fill up the quick form and upload supporting requirements from your phone or computer.

02

Barangay Review

Barangay staff verifies details against database records to validate request legitimacy.

03

Processing

The document is queued, digitally signed, sealed, and approved by officers.

04

Ready for Claim

Receive a calendar invite or SMS notifying you that the document is ready to pick up.

05

Claim & Secure

Head directly to the express desk to present your verification tracking number and grab your file.
